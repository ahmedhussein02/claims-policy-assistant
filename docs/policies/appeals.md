# Appeals

## When you can appeal
You can appeal if your claim is Rejected or if you disagree with the approved amount. You must file the appeal within 30 days of the date on the decision letter. Appeals filed after this period are not reviewed.

## How to appeal
Submit a written appeal through the portal or by mail. Include your claim ID, an explanation of why you disagree, and any new documents or evidence that support your case.

## What happens next
Your appeal is reviewed by a different adjuster from the one who handled the original claim. You will receive a written decision within 20 business days of receipt of your appeal.

## Further review
If you are still unsatisfied after the appeal decision, you may request an independent external review or contact your national insurance regulator. Details are included in the appeal decision letter.

## Tips for a strong appeal
Address the specific reason given in the decision letter, attach evidence that was not available earlier, and keep the explanation short and factual.
