# Coverage Basics

## Types of coverage
Harbor Mutual offers five policy types: Auto, Home, Health, Travel and Life. Your policy schedule lists which coverage you hold, your coverage limits and your deductible.

## Auto coverage
Auto coverage helps pay for repair or replacement of your vehicle after an accident, theft or covered damage, and for liability to others up to the policy limit.

## Home coverage
Home coverage protects the structure of your home and your belongings against covered events such as fire, storm damage, theft and water damage from burst pipes.

## Health coverage
Health coverage reimburses eligible medical expenses such as consultations, hospital stays and prescribed treatment, subject to your annual limit.

## Travel coverage
Travel coverage reimburses covered costs such as trip cancellation, delayed baggage and emergency medical care abroad, subject to the limits on your policy schedule.

## Life coverage
Life coverage pays a lump sum to your named beneficiary when the insured person passes away while the policy is active.

## Deductibles and limits
A deductible is the amount you pay before the insurer pays. A limit is the maximum the insurer will pay for a covered loss. Both are stated on your policy schedule.
