# Required Documents Policy

## General requirements
Every claim needs proof of identity, your policy number and a description of the loss. Additional documents depend on the claim type. Documents may be uploaded as PDF, JPG or PNG files, up to 10 MB each. Documents in another language must be accompanied by a certified translation.

## Auto claims
Auto claims require a police report, a repair estimate from a licensed repairer, and photos of the damage. If another vehicle was involved, include its registration number and the other driver's details.

## Home claims
Home claims require photos of the damage, a contractor estimate for repairs, and proof of ownership such as a title deed or mortgage statement.

## Health claims
Health claims require an itemized medical bill and a doctor's report describing the diagnosis and treatment.

## Travel claims
Travel claims require your travel itinerary, original receipts for the expenses claimed, and an incident report from the carrier, hotel or local authority where applicable.

## Life claims
Life claims require a death certificate and valid identification of the beneficiary.

## Missing documents
If documents are missing, the claim status changes to Action Required. You must upload the missing documents within 14 days to avoid delays in processing. See the Deadlines document for what happens if the 14 days pass.
