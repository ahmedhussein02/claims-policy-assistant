# Exclusions

## General exclusions
The following are not covered under any policy type: intentional or fraudulent acts, losses caused by war or nuclear events, losses that occurred before the policy start date, and losses arising from illegal activity by the policyholder.

## Auto exclusions
Auto policies do not cover normal wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, damage while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or use of the vehicle for racing.

## Home exclusions
Home policies do not cover gradual damage such as rot or rust, flood damage unless flood cover was purchased, or damage from lack of reasonable maintenance.

## Health exclusions
Health policies do not cover cosmetic procedures, experimental treatments, or pre-existing conditions during the waiting period stated in your policy schedule.

## Travel exclusions
Travel policies do not cover travel against government advice, losses from unattended belongings, or cancellation for reasons that were known before the policy was purchased.

## Life exclusions
Life policies may exclude death caused by suicide within the first 12 months of the policy, or by participation in declared high-risk activities.
