# Deadlines

## Filing deadlines
Report an incident as soon as possible. The maximum time to file is 30 days from the incident for Auto, Home and Travel claims, 90 days from the date of treatment for Health claims, and 180 days from the date of death for Life claims. Late claims may be reduced or declined unless you can show a good reason for the delay.

## Document upload deadline
When a claim is in Action Required status you have 14 days to upload the requested documents. If documents are still missing after 14 days, processing is delayed. If nothing is received within 30 days, the claim may be closed, and you would need to file a new claim.

## Review deadline
After all documents are received, we aim to complete review within 10 to 15 business days.

## Payment deadline
Approved claims are paid within 5 to 7 business days.

## Appeal deadline
You have 30 days from the date of the decision letter to file an appeal. See the Appeals document for the process.
