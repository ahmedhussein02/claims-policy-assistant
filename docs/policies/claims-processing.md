# Claims Processing

## Claim statuses
- Submitted: we have received your claim and are assigning an adjuster.
- In Review: an adjuster is assessing your claim. No action is needed from you.
- Action Required: we need more information or documents from you before we can continue.
- Approved: your claim has been accepted and payment is being arranged.
- Rejected: your claim was not accepted. A decision letter explains the reason.

## Processing times
An adjuster is assigned within 2 business days of submission. Once all required documents are received, review typically takes 10 to 15 business days. Complex claims, such as those involving injury or large losses, may take longer and you will be informed of any delay.

## Payment
When a claim is Approved, payment is issued to your registered account within 5 to 7 business days. Payments are made by bank transfer only, and the amount is the approved claim value minus any applicable deductible.

## Adjuster contact
The assigned adjuster may contact you for clarification. Responding promptly keeps your claim moving. Claim status and next steps are always visible in the portal.
