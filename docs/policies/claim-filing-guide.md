# Claim Filing Guide

## How to file a claim
You can file a claim through the customer portal or by calling the claims support line. Each claim receives a claim ID in the format CLM-001, which you should keep for all future communication. Filing through the portal is the fastest option and lets you upload documents immediately.

## Information you need
Have your policy number, the date and location of the incident, a short description of what happened, and contact details ready. For health claims, include the provider name and the date of treatment. For travel claims, include your itinerary dates.

## What happens after you file
After submission your claim has the status Submitted. A claims adjuster is assigned within 2 business days. You will see the status change to In Review once the adjuster begins their assessment.

## Filing for someone else
A family member or authorised representative may file on your behalf if they provide written authorisation and identification for both parties. Claims for Life policies are filed by the named beneficiary.

## Keeping track of your claim
Sign in to the portal at any time to see the status, required documents and next step for each claim. You can only see your own claims.
