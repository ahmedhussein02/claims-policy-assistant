# Policyholder Responsibilities

## Provide accurate information
You must give complete and truthful information when buying a policy and when filing a claim. Providing false or misleading information can lead to a claim being rejected and the policy cancelled.

## Notify us promptly
Report incidents as soon as possible and within the filing deadlines. Delays can make it harder to assess the loss.

## Protect your property
After a loss, take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, for example by covering a broken window or switching off water supply. Keep receipts for emergency repairs, as they may be reimbursed.

## Keep records
Keep photos, receipts, invoices and correspondence related to your claim until it is closed.

## Cooperate with the investigation
Respond to adjuster requests promptly, allow inspections where needed, and provide requested documents within the stated deadlines.

## Keep your details up to date
Make sure your contact information and bank details are current so we can reach you and pay approved claims without delay.
